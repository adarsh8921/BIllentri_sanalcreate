Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about GST compliance, printer drivers, or multi-device sync? Find answers below or contact our 24/7 team.

What is BillEntri and who is it designed for?
BillEntri is an all-in-one GST billing and inventory app crafted for small and medium businesses including retailers, wholesalers, supermarkets, pharmacies, restaurants, and distributors.
Does BillEntri support GST and VAT compliant invoicing?
Yes. BillEntri fully supports GST-compliant invoices with HSN/SAC codes, CGST/SGST/IGST tax breakdowns, tax invoice numbering, and VAT rules for GCC/Overseas businesses.
Can I connect my thermal printer and barcode scanner?
Absolutely. BillEntri is plug-and-play compatible with all 2-inch and 3-inch USB/Bluetooth thermal receipt printers, standard laser/inkjet printers, and barcode scanners. You can download drivers directly from our Drivers & Manuals section.
Can I access my billing data from multiple devices?
Yes. With the Prime Plan, you get real-time multi-device cloud sync across Windows Desktop, Web browser, Android, and iOS devices with automated encrypted cloud backups.
Is my business data secure with BillEntri?
Your data is encrypted with industry-standard 256-bit encryption and backed up continuously on secure cloud servers, ensuring 100% data safety and availability.
