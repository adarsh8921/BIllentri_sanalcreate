Frequently Asked Questions

01 What is BillEntri?
BillEntri is a smart billing and accounting app designed for small businesses. It helps you create invoices, manage customers, track payments, and monitor business performance easily.
02 Who can use BillEntri?
BillEntri is ideal for retailers, wholesalers, service providers, freelancers, and small business owners who want simple and reliable billing without accounting complexity.
03 Does BillEntri support GST billing?
Yes. BillEntri supports GST-compliant invoices with tax breakup, proper invoice numbering, and professional formats suitable for business and accounting needs.
04 Can I track payments and dues?
Absolutely. BillEntri lets you track paid, unpaid, and pending invoices so you always know your outstanding balances and cash flow status.
05 Is BillEntri easy to use?
Yes. BillEntri is built with a clean and user-friendly interface, making billing and accounting easy even for users with no accounting background.
06 Is my business data secure?
Your data is securely stored in the cloud with encryption and regular backups, ensuring complete safety and access anytime, anywhere.
